# Photo register

Every photo used on a project card or sector header must have a row here marked **published**. A photo gets to **verified** only after it has been opened full size and has passed all five checks:

1. **Clean:** a real photograph, with no collage, text overlay, watermark or flyer.
2. **Place:** visible evidence of the location (a signboard, plaque, landmark or LGA name), or the exact site named in the post and backed up by a news source.
3. **Time:** the post date fits the project's documented dates.
4. **Record:** consistent with a sourced fact on the card.
5. **Original:** posted by the first or most credible source.

Status runs: `none` → `candidate` → `verified` → `permission asked` → `approved` → `published` (or `rejected`, with the reason).

Facebook photos belong to the person who posted them. Before one is hosted:

- **Other people's photos:** they need the owner's written permission. The request drafts are in [photo-permission-requests.md](photo-permission-requests.md).
- **The site owner's own photos:** their confirmation in chat is enough.
