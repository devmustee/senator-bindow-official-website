## Message template

> Salam / Good day [name],
>
> I am building a non-commercial archive website documenting the public record of Senator Muhammadu Umaru Jibrilla Bindow, including the projects of his administration in Adamawa State (2015–2019). Every fact on the site is sourced.
>
> Your Facebook post of **[date]** about **[project]** has clear photographs of the work. May we publish **[that photo / those photos]** on the site?
>
> Each photo would carry the credit **"Photo: [name], [date]. Used with permission."** and a link to your original post. We would not edit the photos beyond resizing. You can ask us to remove them at any time.
>
> If you agree, a short reply such as "Yes, you may use them with credit" is enough. Thank you.
